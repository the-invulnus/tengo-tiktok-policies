Tengo TikTok Sandbox

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Tengo is testing an integration between an AI video creation and editing platform and TikTok. This site provides the policies for that integration test.

The current local test connects an authorized TikTok account and queries creator information. It does not upload or publish videos.
